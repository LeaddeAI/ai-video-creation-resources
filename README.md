## Example workflow

A simple AI video creation workflow usually looks like this:

1. Prepare source material, such as a PDF, Word document, slide deck, or script.
2. Convert the material into a structured video script.
3. Choose an AI avatar, voice, layout, and visual style.
4. Generate the video.
5. Review, edit, and export the final version.

This workflow is useful for online courses, internal training, product tutorials, and educational content.
